Privacy Policy

This is the public version of the LUTAS LED Privacy Policy, available without signing in.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, and the profile details you choose to give when you sign up.
  • Your designs — the layouts and projects you save to your account, and their titles.
  • Shares — client links you create, their settings, and comments or reviews left on them. Names on comments are typed by the person posting and are not verified.
  • Anonymous visit counts — when the app opens, a visit is counted with a hashed identifier, the visitor's country, the device type, which view was opened, and how long the page stayed open. These counts appear in the admin Visitors section. No raw IP address is stored — the IP is hashed on the server and discarded.
  • Card details — never. Payments are handled by PayHere (LKR) or Paddle (USD); card details go to them and are never stored in LUTAS LED.

How we use it

We use this data to run the service: signing you in, saving and loading your designs, operating your client shares, managing your plan, answering your messages, and counting anonymous visits so we can understand how the app is used. We do not sell your personal data.

Where it is stored

Account details, designs, shares and visit records are stored in Supabase, the database service LUTAS LED runs on. Payment details are held by PayHere or Paddle under their own privacy policies, not by us.

Visits and your account

A visit stays anonymous unless you sign in during it — then that visit is linked to your account, so the Visitors section can tell a signed-in visit from a guest visit. Guests are never identified by name or email.

Asking for your data to be deleted

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, at any time. Email hello@lutas.digital from your account email and we will delete your account data as requested.
