Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Cancel at any time

You can cancel a paid plan (Advance or VIP) at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal — you are not charged again.

What happens after you cancel

  • Your paid access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • When that period ends, your plan drops back to Starter (free).
  • Your designs are kept — nothing you created is deleted. The paid tools simply lock again until you upgrade.

14-day refund rule

The refund rule below is the one that applies to your payment:

  • You can get a refund within 14 days of payment, if the paid features have not been used.
  • There is no refund after 14 days, and no refund once the paid features have been used — even inside the 14 days.

The same rule applies to monthly and yearly payments. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment processor (PayHere for LKR, Paddle for USD) to the payment method you paid with.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@lutas.digital within the 14 days, from or naming your account email, with the date of the payment. We will confirm whether the paid features were used and reply about your refund.
